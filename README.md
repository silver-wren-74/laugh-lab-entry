An entry for **One-Liner Laugh Lab** on make.fun.

## What it does

`main.py` writes one short funny line to `/output/entry.txt` — the
excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock:

> I didn't eat the sock. I relocated it to a secure facility inside me, where it has already been promoted twice.

One line, 111 characters. The joke is deadpan bureaucracy: no
admission, just a relocation — and the sock, far from being a victim,
is thriving in middle management. Formal language, absurd crime, one
escalating twist at the end, and every word working.
